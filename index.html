Side B · Product thinking

The studio sessions.

pick a tape, hear how I think through a product

Ticket triage, remixedKeck Medicine · ServiceNow

The problem

ServiceNow triage was manual and repetitive, and it created bottlenecks for the engineering teams waiting on it.

The insight

A systematic process brings real joy. Automating the repetitive sorting gives engineers their time back for the work that matters.

What I built

An MCP server that summarizes each ticket, routes it, sets priority and groups duplicates together.

Trade-off I made

Automate every repetitive step end to end, and leave people only the decisions that need their judgment.

The result

84% less triage time and fewer bottlenecks for engineering.
